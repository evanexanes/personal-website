About Me

Hi! I’m Evan Exanes, a tech enthusiast currently in my first year of college, taking up Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT). I got my start in ICT back in high school, and ever since then, I’ve been hooked on everything tech-related.

Right now, I’m diving into WordPress — learning how to design and build websites from scratch.
